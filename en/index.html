    Frequently asked questions

    How much is a men's haircut?

    A buzz cut is $30, a classic haircut is $45 and a skin fade is $50. Every cut includes a shampoo and styling.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Yes, when a chair is open. Booking online guarantees your time and your barber.

    Do you cut kids' hair?

    Yes. Kids' cuts (12 and under) are $35, and the Father & son package is two cuts for $70.

    How do I reschedule or cancel?

    Use the link in your confirmation or tap "My bookings" in our bot. Please cancel at least 2 hours ahead.

    Is tipping included?

    No, tips are not included in our prices. You can tip by card or cash — it all goes to your barber.

    Do you sell gift cards?

    Yes, in any amount from $40 or for a specific service. Buy them in the shop or get an e-gift card by email.
